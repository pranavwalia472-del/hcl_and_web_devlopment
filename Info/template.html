MediConnect · A little design research

Finding a look for the website

So before building the pages, I looked around for healthcare and telemedicine website designs. I wanted something clean and modern, with a good way to present doctors and make appointment booking feel straightforward. Here are some of the places I checked and a few designs I shortlisted.

Where I looked

A mix of ready-made website templates and UI design inspiration.

Canva Figma Community Dribbble Behance ThemeForest Dorik

Canva and template marketplaces were useful for complete website examples. Figma, Dribbble, and Behance were useful for exploring individual screens, layouts, and UI ideas.

A few candidates I considered

Dorik · Wellbe My choice

Wellbe – Telehealth Website

A telehealth-focused design with a teal palette, clear hero section, service highlights, and healthcare-focused content.

Why I picked it: The overall look felt right for MediConnect: calm, modern, and professional. The teal direction was a good fit, and the layout gave me ideas for presenting services and guiding users toward booking without making the site feel crowded.

View the Wellbe template ↗
DoccureAlternative

Doccure – Doctor Appointment

A more feature-heavy healthcare website concept, with doctor search, specialties, doctor profiles, and appointment-related sections.

What I liked: It showed useful ways to organize doctor discovery and appointment booking. I kept it as a reference for features and page structure, but its overall design was not the visual direction I wanted to use.

View the Doccure demo ↗
HealoraAlternative

Healora – Online Consultation

A teal-and-green online consultation design with doctor imagery, service information, and a strong landing-page layout.

What I considered: It was relevant to the telemedicine idea and had a polished look. I preferred Wellbe's simpler visual direction for the version of MediConnect I was building.

View the Healora template ↗

So, I went with Wellbe.

It was the closest match to what I had in mind. I liked the teal and dark tones, clean layout, and healthcare-focused feel. It also gave me a useful starting point without forcing me to use features or layouts that the project did not need.

I didn't copy the template as-is. I used it as a visual reference and adapted the pages, colors, and components around MediConnect's own features: Home, Doctors, Book Appointment, My Appointments, FAQ, and Contact.

The previews above are examples from the linked template pages. They are included to show the kind of designs I compared, not to suggest that the final MediConnect interface is an exact copy.
